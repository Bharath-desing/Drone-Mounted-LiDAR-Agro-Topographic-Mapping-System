01 — Processing Chain

From raw returns to farm-ready zones

Six stages, each mirroring what a production agro-LiDAR pipeline does with real flight data — this one just starts from a physically-plausible simulated survey instead of a physical drone.

Stage 01

Flight Simulation

Multi-swath drone survey generated with overlapping scan lines, ~55% direct ground penetration, and 3cm sensor noise — matching real survey-grade LiDAR characteristics.

Stage 02

Noise Filtering

Statistical outlier removal via k-nearest-neighbor distance, discarding sensor noise and stray returns before classification.

Stage 03

Ground Classification

A progressive morphological filter separates bare-earth returns from canopy — the same family of algorithm used in PDAL and commercial lasground tools.

Stage 04

Surface Modeling

Interpolates classified points into DTM (bare earth), DSM (first surface), and CHM (canopy height) raster products.

Stage 05

Topographic Analysis

Derives slope, aspect, curvature, D8 flow accumulation, and Topographic Wetness Index from the terrain model.

Stage 06

Agronomic Interpretation

Translates topography into erosion risk, drainage risk, and variable-rate irrigation zone maps ready for a farm management system.

03 — Methodology

Built on established algorithms

Each stage implements a documented, citable method rather than an ad-hoc heuristic.

Progressive Morphological Filter

Increasing-window grayscale erosion with a slope-adaptive elevation threshold separates ground from canopy returns.

Zhang et al., 2003 — IEEE TGRS

D8 Flow Routing

Each cell drains to its steepest of 8 neighbors; accumulation propagates from highest to lowest elevation to trace drainage lines.

Standard algorithm — ArcHydro / GRASS r.watershed

Topographic Wetness Index

ln(contributing area / tan(slope)) — a widely-used proxy for soil moisture and waterlogging risk in digital soil mapping.

Beven & Kirkby, 1979

Variable-Rate Zoning

A smoothed composite of normalized slope and TWI is binned into contiguous management zones for irrigation prescriptions.

Standard precision-ag VRI construction
